About

Where law meets code

Uganda's legal sector runs on paper, and paper is slow to search, easy to lose, and hard to secure. I build the software that closes that gap data-protection tooling and contract automation that make legal work faster and safer.

I'm in my second year of a BSc in Software Engineering, running a parallel track as a freelance developer and the founder of Nigel Tech Solutions. Outside legal tech, I take on full-stack builds for small businesses because shipping real products for real clients is the fastest way to get better at all of it.

Long-term, I'm building toward defense adjacent and systems level engineering the kind of work where correctness and security aren't optional.
