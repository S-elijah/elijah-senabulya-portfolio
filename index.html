About

From packets to evidence

I'm a Software Engineering student at Victoria University Kampala, building toward a career as a Junior Network & Digital Forensics Engineer.

My focus is networking and digital forensics (DFIR). I work on Linux, build and test networks in Cisco Packet Tracer, and use Wireshark to analyse traffic, with Python and database fundamentals for scripting and handling data. The software engineering degree is the foundation underneath.

I'm also interested in how this applies to law and legal systems in Uganda, and I write about it in my Legal Tech Uganda newsletter and short TikTok explainers. On the side, I run Nigel Tech Solutions and I'm a member of the Patriotic League of Uganda (PC-18158). I'm open to internships, junior roles, and collaborations in networking, digital forensics, and legal tech.
